Urban heat and health risks in Jashore and Chuadanga

M.Sc. thesis, Gopalganj Science and Technology University

OngoingArcGISGoogle Earth EngineSPSSRemote sensing

Spatio-temporal assessment of Urban Heat Island intensity and the heat-related health risks it creates, with a focus on nature-based solutions and urban heat resilience.

  • Analyzing Urban Heat Island and Land Surface Temperature from Landsat imagery, 2010 to 2025.
  • Linking heat patterns to land use and land cover change using NDVI, NDBI, and NDWI.
